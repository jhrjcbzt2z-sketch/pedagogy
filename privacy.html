Petagogy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Petagogy publishes educational videos by Professor Truffle to its own TikTok account.

The Petagogy app connects only to the account owned by its operator. It uses TikTok Login and the Content Posting API to upload those videos. It does not offer sign-up to the public and does not collect, sell or share data about viewers.

The access tokens issued by TikTok are stored privately on the operator's computer and are used only to publish videos. They can be revoked at any time from the TikTok account settings.

Contact: youtube.com/@petagogy
